Privacy Policy

How JN Couture Salon handles the information you send us.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

What we collect

When you use the contact form on this site, we receive the name, email address, phone number and message you enter, plus the service and stylist you pick. We use it only to reply to you and to schedule your appointment.

How it reaches us

Form messages are delivered to our salon email inbox through FormSubmit, an email forwarding service. We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone else.

Site statistics

We count page visits with privacy-friendly analytics that don't use cookies and don't identify you personally.

Questions

If you'd like us to delete a message you sent, or you have any questions, contact JN Couture Salon at jncouturesalon@gmail.com or (208) 762-4667, 8879 N Hess St, Suite C, Hayden, ID 83835.
